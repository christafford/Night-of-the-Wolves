The hermit and bandit dialogue uses original scripts rendered locally with Carnegie Mellon Flite 2.2 and its bundled kal16 voice. The audio uses two pitch and duration profiles. Window and pit lines use low-pass filtering to simulate distance through timber and mud. These are temporary synthetic performances. The game's runtime plays the rendered mono WAVs.

The source includes the license supplied with the Arch Linux build of Flite. Re-render with `python3 Scripts/render_first_leg_dialogue.py` using Flite and ffmpeg on PATH. [Flite and licensing](https://github.com/festvox/flite)
