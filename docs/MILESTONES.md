## First leg

The saved startup map carries the original trail into a playable five-encounter route. The first-leg brief supersedes the early plan to finish at the cottage. Progression now continues through the barred cottage, firebrand decision, logging cableway, robbery and escape bend.

| Increment | Playable outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Shared equipment and retry | One equipped item, paged wheel, full pause, actual-item checkpoint snapshots and local resume |
| Cottage revision | A visible armed rush to the rotten cover on both branches, a timed dig, a bare-handed knock, a barred-door refuge with separate rope and gold pickups, and a firebrand that clears the waiting pack |
| Pack encounters | Five persistent wolves share sightings, follow physical routes and change assignments. One can earn the pounce while the others apply pressure |
| Creek and bandit | Washed-out bridge, rope cast over a raised logging trolley hook, downhill ride to the far landing, a look back at the pack hesitating and swimming downstream, an aimed throw that spends the sack, a fatal failed robbery, and an escape window after the wolves bring down the bandit |
| Pacific Northwest forest | Instanced canopy and undergrowth, vegetation exclusions around the puzzle spaces, dressed banks and a readable trail |
| Linux verification | Keyboard and mouse play from the trailhead through the escape bend in Shipping, cold resume from the barred cottage, plus development runs for the death and retry branches |

The evidence for each increment is in [PLAYTEST.md](PLAYTEST.md).
