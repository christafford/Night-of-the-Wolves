# Night of the Wolves: game direction

## The experience

A third-person survival game set outdoors in a forest at night. The unnamed protagonist, provisionally called Max, has no backstory. He begins alone on a trail, visible in light from the full moon, with wolf howls and forest sounds providing ambience. A wolf pack pursues him throughout the game. The goal is to survive until daylight, running and solving environmental puzzles under pressure; wolf capture and other fatal outcomes return the player to the last checkpoint.

Max can try each encounter as many times as he wants. Context text describes what he notices. Control prompts name the action he can take with his hands or the item he has equipped. Let the player decide what an observation means and what to try on the next attempt.

The forest uses realistic dark woodland, blue-gray moonlight, mist, tangled branches and mossy ground. Firelight escapes through the hermit’s single cabin window. The cabin itself is built of logs with a stone hearth. [Atmospheric reference](https://media.easy-peasy.ai/b535d61a-ed9a-48fb-90d0-329997e25ef5/4e7998ac-d2dd-4dd7-9bd7-4ffb57562189.png)

For the trail, the user also supplied [a reference](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRnGPlMPUJ7rfeaCfjlPE4JIdZE3ZlofdCGczORUhglChtq_4RN8vHHkJc&s=10) showing uneven, mossy stone in a dense moonlit conifer forest. The aim is a lifelike path in a close, enclosing mix of trees.

Max wears a dark blue trail jacket with ochre shoulders and elbows, a gray shirt, dark jeans and oxblood sneakers. His brown fringe, light stubble and lean build follow the supplied runner photograph. [Visual reference](https://as2.ftcdn.net/jpg/02/06/28/07/1000_F_206280746_q9eKQ3nva9cuhLm3Um5fA8zkFZPI0drg.jpg)

## The Moonlit Trail

A fresh trail opens with “Night of the Wolves” and “A Stafford Laboratory Production.” The title dissolves into the silver alpha’s view from the start of the path. Four packmates run ahead. The stride and close breathing belong to the alpha. The camera peels away to reveal the silver wolf coming through to take point, then settles behind Max. The score “No Birds at This Hour” enters as Max gains control. A long bowed contrabass note sits under the forest sounds. Cellos descend by a semitone, high strings tremble, and a few muffled drum strokes break the quiet.

The first leg runs from the opening chase to the bend beyond the bandit. The five encounters, equipment rules, checkpoint snapshots and failure cases come from [the first-leg brief](../mynotes/NIGHT_OF_THE_WOLVES_FIRST_LEG.md). It replaces the earlier prototype ending at the cottage.

- **Trail.** Max starts with empty hands and a five-second lead once control is available. Five individual wolves keep hunting across the whole leg. The heavy, dark pursuer closes from behind. The silver leader watches for a change and signals. The pale-faced flanker looks for an open shoulder, the long-legged scout can take a second route, and the tracker lowers its nose when the pack loses sight. Moonlight picks out the pale muzzle and silver-tipped cheek ruff against a dark brow, echoing the wolf on the death screen. A sighting or audible footfall can bring the pack back onto the trail. On capture, the wolf that reached a clear launch position springs onto Max before the static death image appears. A close snarl hits as the card comes up. Running through a gap in fallen timber buys time to investigate the cottage.
- **Doorstep.** A shovel stands among a barrel and a bale of hay outside the handmade log cabin. Max grips the spade with both hands, plants the blade and levers out the rotten cover over three strokes. To knock on the timber door, he must select Bare hands in the equipment wheel. The hermit warns Max from behind the glowing window in a low, rough voice. The camera stays with Max. On a knock, the hermit runs outside, plants his feet on the rotten timbers and turns to shout “I warned you!” A close-up shows his face under the creased felt hat, with the beard and work overalls inspired by Popcorn Sutton. If the cover is intact, he shoots Max. If Max cleared it with the shovel, the hermit slips, catches briefly at the rim and pleads for help. The view widens as he loses his grip. He lets out the Wilhelm scream, his shotgun goes off as he hits the bottom, and he lies still. Max can knock from either jamb and must jump the hollow to enter and bar the door.
- **Refuge.** Scratching and impacts build against the barred door. A sack of gold sits on the worktable. Across the room, a coil of rope hangs on a peg on the back wall. Max must approach and take each separately, in either order. His equipped item stays in his hand when he picks one up. The pack waits just outside, and Max can see it by opening the door. Crossing that threshold without the burning firebrand in hand triggers an immediate attack. Holding the flame makes the wolves retreat from the landing. Max must jump the hollow again to get outside. Max can return to the hearth to relight it.
- **Crossing.** Falling into the current triggers a Wilhelm scream. Max reaches for the surface, kicks and drifts downstream before he sinks and retries. Floodwater has taken out the bridge, but an old logging cable still spans the creek. The trolley’s upturned iron hook hangs four metres above the loading platform. Max puts away the lit firebrand and casts a loop of cabin rope over the hook. The rope settles into a hand sling. The wheel runs downhill when he kicks off. Letting go over the current costs him his life. He can drop onto the far landing as it comes underneath him or wait for the end stop before stepping off. The trolley and sling stay across the creek. On landing, the camera looks back to the broken bridge. The lead wolf checks the gap, turns downstream with the pack and starts swimming across. The view returns to Max while the wolves are still in the water. Resuming a far-bank checkpoint returns directly to Max.
- **Robbery.** The roadside bandit takes his appearance from the supplied portrait of Pancho Villa. His broad felt brim tips back above a heavy, parted moustache, and crossed leather bandoliers stand out against his light campaign shirt. He steps into the road and demands Max's possessions. The camera moves in for his measured demand in Mexican-accented English. When Max shows the gold, the bandit lowers his eyes to the sack, lifts his brows and smiles. His voice turns almost friendly and slows on the word “slowly.” His mouth follows each recorded line. Max can turn out of the close-up to aim the throw. With the sack equipped, the only action prompt is “Throw gold.” Every throw spends the sack. It travels where Max aims and can land on either side of the road. If the sack lands in the path of the approaching pack, the robber goes for the coins and gets between Max and the wolves. He hears them and looks up. On a miss, he keeps Max covered and fires while the pack continues closing. Trying to run around the living robber also draws his fire. A far-bank retry restores the sack. The camera holds on his face, then pulls back so Max can see the pack bring him down. His pain cry lands with the attack. Once the wolves bring him down, control returns to Max and his escape window begins. The leg ends beyond the next bend while it is still night.

Ordinary pickups add to an inventory of unlimited capacity. One item can be equipped. The paged inventory wheel pauses the simulation. Confirming a change away from a burning firebrand extinguishes it. Canceling keeps both the item and flame. Rigging the trolley spends the rope. Releasing the sack spends the gold wherever it lands.

Checkpoints fall at the start, cottage approach, barred door, near bank, far bank and escape bend. Each checkpoint captures the items actually carried. The barred-door checkpoint restores the hearth brand and the pack outside. Older saves that already passed the cottage retain their recorded inventory. The shovel and spent firebrand carry forward at the end of a new run.

## The storm and cave

After Max escapes the bandit, lightning splits a fir ahead. The trunk falls across the logging road and burns. The flash catches the five wolves mid-pursuit. As the tree ignites, they recoil, turn and run back down the road. A short cut to the pack shows them retreating. They keep their distance while the tree burns, giving Max time to explore even with an unlit brand. Max can bring his spent firebrand to the glowing wood and relight it. The cave mouth opens to one side of the road.

The cave’s first chamber ends at a rockfall. Holding the flame reveals the tangled dry roots screening a narrow side passage. Max can touch the flame to the roots. They burn through, and walking into the inner chamber earns CP6. An extinguished brand cannot clear the roots. Stowing the lit brand before touching the roots means returning for fire. Older saves from the retired grate scene resume at the preceding bend with their carried items.

## Tuning and later work

The five-second opening lead is fixed. The reach of a successful diversion, fire visibility on different displays and the pace of the bandit’s distraction are playtest values. The inner chamber is the current playable endpoint. The deeper cave puzzle, eventual return to the trail and timing of dawn remain open.
