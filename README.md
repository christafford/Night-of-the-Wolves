# Night of the Wolves

A single-player, third-person survival game set in a moonlit Pacific Northwest forest. The playable first leg begins on the trail and follows the pursuit through a hermit's cottage, a washed-out crossing and a roadside robbery.

## Play on Linux

Launch the Moonlit Trail Shipping build locally.

```sh
"$HOME/Documents/Unreal Projects/Night_of_the_Wolves/Saved/MoonlitTrail-Linux/Linux/Night_of_the_Wolves.sh"
```

A new trail opens from the lead wolf’s point of view. To watch it from the beginning while keeping your saved checkpoint, append `-NoTrailSave` to the launch command.

Run with **WASD**, look with the **mouse**, hold **Left Shift** to sprint, and press **Space** to jump. **E** interacts with the world and **F** uses the equipped item. At the crossing, **E** grips the attached rope and **E** again releases it.

**Tab** opens the inventory wheel and pauses the game. Point at an item or use the arrow keys, then click or press **Enter** to equip it. Right-click, **Esc**, or **Tab** cancels. A confirmed switch away from the burning firebrand extinguishes it.

**Esc** opens the pause menu. **R** retries from the latest checkpoint. The game also resumes a saved checkpoint on the next launch. Choose **N**, then **Enter**, to start a new trail from the beginning.
